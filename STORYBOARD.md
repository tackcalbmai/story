# Storyboard v1 — ~3:20

The public-facing piece is deliberately sparse. This board describes what the viewer should *feel* and what the frame is doing, not just what text appears.

| Time | Beat | Visual / camera | Sound / silence | Function |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:18 | Cold open continuation | After the gate `И так… / НЕЕЕТ`, split bedroom / car. Slow push. `Я вообще-то ничего ещё не сказала` → `Я знаю`. Title arrives late. | very low room/road bed, tiny notification tones | She recognises the piece before the title; strangers understand instinctive familiarity. |
| 0:18–0:42 | Road ↔ kitchen | Diagonal split. Road lights and kitchen lamps visually rhyme. Camera drift, then hard comic punch on `ПОДАЧА!`. | road rumble → kitchen texture → single low impact → brief comic air | Gives the relationship life and texture; prevents solemnity from becoming syrup. |
| 0:42–1:04 | Shared minutes | Five memory cards: morning, food word, road calls, `малыш`, bedtime shorthand. Cards float like fragments rather than screenshots. | restrained clicks/chimes, no score | Shows embedding into everyday life without pretending exact dates. |
| 1:04–1:31 | Armor | Day half: `Всё нормально`. Night half: voice line, blanket, `Нет` → `Враки`. Hold after the tiny laugh. | almost dry; subtle call-wave texture | Central intimacy beat: being allowed to stop performing strength. |
| 1:31–1:50 | Winter | Cold planes, small music player, warmth blooms from inside the frame. Her real reaction arrives in layers. | first actual musical-emotional lift; real song can replace fallback | He sees the version she does not normally show. Winter → warmth becomes the visual motif for the whole film. |
| 1:50–2:07 | First `люблю` | Parked car. No movement except waveform. When the word lands, the camera barely advances. | strip almost everything away before the word | Irreversible point. Stillness instead of melodrama. |
| 2:07–2:29 | Return to now | Her schedule crowds left side; his bag/course preparation crowds right. Message read later. Space physically tightens. | busier noise but lower emotional score | Makes the problem external: less time, not less feeling. |
| 2:29–2:54 | `Потом` | Two worlds remain split. Dialogue alternates across the divide. `Я знаю` mirrors the beginning. `Это же общее желание` is the only statement allowed to sit centered. | quiet, no emotional swell until after the line | The dramatic argument: he wants certainty; she chooses the present. Film sides with neither and promises nothing. |
| 2:54–3:20 | Sweet dreams / open end | Her goodnight + kiss sticker, his longer reply + nose-kiss sticker. Then both spaces dissolve into darkness. Two impossible light traces remain between far windows. | two small chimes → near-silence | No time-jump, no breakup, no reunion. Future stays unresolved; influence does not. |
| after | Birthday epilogue | Pure black, then one short personal card. | silence | Returns from “film” to “gift” without explaining the ending. |

## Cut criteria
A beat stays only if it does at least one of these:
- changes our understanding of the bond;
- raises pressure on the bond;
- gives emotional release;
- creates a later callback;
- contains a personal code that also works for a stranger.

Everything else is reserve material.
