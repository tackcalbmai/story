# Directing bible

## Core dramatic question
Not “will they meet?” They almost certainly will not. The film asks: **can a bond remain real when circumstances keep taking away the time and space in which it lives?**

## Chronology
The film is **not** a fake chronological reconstruction of every real event.

It has a clear frame:
1. **Now:** the familiar “И так…” → “НЕЕЕТ” opens the piece.
2. **Accumulated memory:** road calls, kitchen, language, vulnerability, song, first “люблю”. These are remembered fragments of how the bond became real; their internal montage does not claim dates we do not know.
3. **Back to now:** her current overload + his coming course period compress the time available to them.
4. **The future question:** he reaches for “потом”; she refuses to live there early.
5. **Tonight:** the sweet-dreams ritual closes the present frame.
6. **Open future:** no time-jump tells us whether they keep speaking. Only the visual trace remains.

That keeps the chronology honest: known present → memory field → known present. We do not invent a false order between private events whose exact dates are unknown.

## What the film must never do
- Never put both characters in the same physical location, even in fantasy.
- Never promise a future the characters themselves cannot promise.
- Never turn the ending into a pre-written breakup.
- Never use the private details as a checklist of Easter eggs; each detail must carry story function.
- Never explain why meeting is impossible. That would make the audience solve logistics instead of feel the relationship.

## Visual grammar
Two real spaces + one impossible shared space.

Her world: cool blue-grey, restaurant tungsten, bedroom darkness.
His world: road black, dashboard amber, parking-lot sodium light.

When they connect, light/sound crosses the split. When life compresses the bond, those match-cuts fail and both frames feel physically separate again.

## Emotional progression
1. **Recognition / ease** — “И так…” → “НЕЕЕТ”. He knows the next sentence before she writes it.
2. **Private world** — road + restaurant; “ПОДАЧА!” proves intimacy can be funny and unpolished.
3. **Embedding** — “малыш”, “почавкала”, good mornings. One person begins living inside the other’s language.
4. **Being seen** — she can stop performing strength with him; “Враки” breaks the armor without grand speeches.
5. **Naming what he sees** — “Вечная зима”. The song is not a music-video insert; it is proof he sees beneath the public version of her.
6. **Irreversible point** — first “люблю” in voice. No melodramatic reaction; stillness is the reaction.
7. **External pressure / return to now** — less time, not less feeling.
8. **Future conflict** — he reaches for “потом”; she refuses to live there early. “Я знаю” mirrors the opening and turns their playful code into dramatic structure.
9. **Ritual** — sweet dreams + two stickers. Ordinary repetition carries more weight than a farewell speech.
10. **Open end / closed meaning** — no answer about what happens later. The only closed statement is visual: each world retains an impossible trace of the other.

## Personal codes used deliberately
- “И так…” / “НЕЕЕТ” — opening, callback, knowledge without explanation.
- “малыш” — shared language.
- road calls — where their spoken world exists.
- “ПОДАЧА!” — release valve; prevents three minutes of uninterrupted solemnity.
- blanket hiding — physical expression of shyness/overwhelm.
- “Враки” — his word adopted into her language and a gentle way of seeing through the mask.
- “Вечная зима” — he sees her hidden burden; winter-to-spring motif drives the light design.
- first “люблю” in voice — emotional midpoint / irreversible beat.
- “Это же общее желание” — thesis, but delivered as her real sentence, not screenwriter philosophy.
- sweet dreams + sticker exchange — closing ritual.

## Codes intentionally held back
“Приветик” conflict, birthday “ну ты и старьё”, and several other details stay out of v1. Three minutes cannot carry every memory. They remain reserve material if testing reveals a weak beat.

## Sound
Silence is a character. Do not wallpaper the full runtime with music.

- road: low filtered rumble
- restaurant: brighter noise bed + one strong comic transient on “ПОДАЧА!”
- armor: almost no score
- winter: first recognisable musical motif
- love: drop music before “люблю”
- final ritual: tiny notification chimes, then near-silence

If the real voice note and real song are added, use the shortest emotionally sufficient excerpts.

## Editing rule
If a beautiful shot does not change feeling, information, pressure or recognition, cut it.
